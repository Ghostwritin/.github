## Your words. No fingerprints.

A tool helped with your first draft, and now it doesn't sound like you. **Ghostwritin'** rewrites
AI-assisted drafts into your own natural voice, then checks that the meaning stayed exactly the same.

- **Your voice.** Casual, Professional, Academic, or My voice, learned from three to five of your own samples.
- **Meaning locked.** Names, numbers, quotes and code never move, and each rewrite is checked against your original.
- **Every change on the record.** A diff shows what was cut and what was written, with a human score before and after.

Open source (MIT) once it ships: self-host with your own model key, or use the hosted version.
Web app, REST API, CLI and MCP server on one engine.
